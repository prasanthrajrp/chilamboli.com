Chilamboli Dance Academy is devoted to the practice, preservation and celebration of Indian classical dance. Inspired by Kerala’s artistic heritage, the academy creates a space where students can experience the depth of classical movement and the joy of performance.

Dance is more than steps and patterns. It brings together rhythm, expression, posture, storytelling and dedication. Our approach values strong foundations, regular practice and a deep respect for each dance tradition.

From a beginner discovering the first mudras to a dedicated student preparing for the stage, every learner’s journey matters.
