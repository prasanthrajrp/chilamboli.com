THE CHILAMBOLI STORY

A home for
classical arts.

Chilamboli Dance Academy, based in Parassala, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, is devoted to the practice, preservation and celebration of Indian classical dance.

Founded with a love for the performing arts, the academy encourages students to build strong foundations in technique, rhythm, expression and stage confidence. Every class is an opportunity to connect with culture and grow through disciplined practice.

Our approach honours the depth of each classical form while making the learning journey inspiring and welcoming for students.

With grace, discipline & devotion.
